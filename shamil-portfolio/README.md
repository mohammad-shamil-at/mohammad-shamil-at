# Mohammad Shamil AT — Portfolio

An ivory, charcoal, and orange editorial portfolio with a typography-led introduction, wide project rows, expertise panels, career timeline, and contact section. No avatars or video assets.

## Preview
Extract the ZIP and open index.html. Keep assets/ and src/ beside it.

## GitHub Pages
Upload all extracted contents to your repository root, including index.html, assets/, src/, and .nojekyll. Do not upload the ZIP itself.
In Settings > Pages, choose Deploy from a branch, main, and /(root), then Save.

## Features
Light/dark theme, mobile menu, project links, interactive process panels, reduced-motion support, and email draft form. The form opens an email application; it does not send messages or store submissions.

## Content
Based on Shamil’s supplied resume. Passport details, date of birth, and unfinished metrics are excluded. Fonts retained from the supplied reference. External project and profile links are from the resume.

## Checks
JavaScript syntax, local asset paths, section anchors, unique IDs, and ZIP integrity checked. Avatar markup, controls, and media removed. Browser visual/interaction verification and deployment remain unavailable in this workspace.
